# Nursa

Nursa is a healthcare staffing platform that connects nursing clinicians seeking flexible, per diem work with healthcare facilities that need to fill shifts. Founded in 2019 and based in Salt Lake City, Utah, Nursa's mission is "a nurse at the bedside of every patient in need." The platform serves over 400,000 verified clinicians and 3,400+ facilities across 31+ states.

## About

Nursa is a marketplace platform that enables healthcare facilities and qualified clinicians to connect to directly, bypassing traditional staffing agencies and reducing costs. Facilities post shifts, set their own rates, review clinician profiles, and select candidates—all through the platform. Clinicians add their credentials and experience, browse and request shifts that fit their schedule, and receive payment after completing shifts. Nursa operates on a pay-as-you-go model with transparent pricing, no long-term contracts, no minimum posted shift requirements, and no hire-away fees.

The platform supports Registered Nurses (RNs), Licensed Practical Nurses (LPNs/LVNs), Certified Nursing Assistants (CNAs), Certified Medication Aides (CMAs), and other allied healthcare professionals. It serves acute-care hospitals, skilled nursing facilities, long-term care, rehabilitation centers, memory care, and more post-acute care settings.
